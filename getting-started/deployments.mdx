title: "Deployments"
description: "Base URLs, and where your events are kept on Orchid's cloud, a firm VM or on premises."

The developer API is the same in every deployment: the same paths, fields and answers. Only the address and where your data is kept differ.

| Deployment | Base URL | Where your events and keys are kept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Orchid's cloud | `https://app.orchid.ac/v1` | Orchid's central database, kept apart by firm with row-level security |
| Firm VM | your install's own address, such as `https://orchid.yourfirm.com/v1` | your own database on the VM |
| On premises | your gateway's address on your network | your own database |

On a firm VM or on premises, keys are checked in your own database, and nothing you send through the developer API reaches Orchid's cloud.

## Editions

Orchid runs as one of two editions:

- **Full**: Orchid answers questions itself, routes models and runs AI employees.
- **Monitor**: Claude or ChatGPT answers, and Orchid checks and records each request.

Everything in these docs works in both editions, except [AI employee runs](/act/agent-runs) and [KPIs](/read/cost#kpis), which need the full edition. In the monitor edition those routes do not exist (`404`).

## Webhook addresses

On Orchid's cloud, webhooks are only sent to public addresses (`https`, resolving to a public IP), checked when the webhook is made and again before each delivery. On a firm VM or on premises, Orchid sends wherever your network allows.

## Database updates

The developer API's read side, scopes and webhooks need two database updates, applied by Orchid with each release:

- `0101_developer_api` on Orchid's cloud;
- `0041_developer_api` in a firm's own database.

Until a firm's database has been updated, keys work with the default scopes (send, and read back your own), and making a key with more scopes is refused with a message saying so.
