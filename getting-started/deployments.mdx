---
title: "Deployments"
description: "Base URLs on Orchid's cloud, a firm VM or on premises."
---

The developer API is the same in every deployment: the same paths, fields and answers. Only the address differs.

| Deployment | Base URL |
| --- | --- |
| Orchid's cloud | `https://app.orchid.ac/v1` |
| Firm VM | your install's own address, such as `https://orchid.yourfirm.com/v1` |
| On premises | your gateway's address on your network |

On a firm VM or on premises, your events stay in your own database.

## Webhook addresses

On Orchid's cloud, webhooks are only sent to public `https` addresses. On a firm VM or on premises, Orchid sends wherever your network allows.
